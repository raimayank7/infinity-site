URL Shortener: Make Long Links Short

Turn a long link into a short v.gd link that is easy to share, with an optional custom ending. Free, no sign-up, and links never expire.

How to shorten a link

  1. Paste your long link.
  2. Optionally type a custom ending.
  3. Click Shorten link and copy the result.

Questions

Is it free?

Completely free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit on how many times you use it. Light ads keep the site running.

Do short links expire?

No. Links made here keep working for good.

Where are my links saved?

Your recent links are listed in your own browser only. Clearing the list does not stop the links working.
